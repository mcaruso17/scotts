## Key points / insights

- *The Remix* expands the widely used *Causal Inference: The Mixtape* into a fuller econometrics foundation (~784 pp.).
- It covers the core design-based toolkit: unconfoundedness/selection-on-observables, instrumental variables, regression discontinuity, and panel methods (fixed effects, difference-in-differences, synthetic control).
- The difference-in-differences treatment is substantially extended, from fundamentals through staggered adoption and covariate adjustment.
- The online version launched as a living, work-in-progress document that will keep gaining content over time.
- A planned essay explains how to read/use the book alongside LLMs, reflecting Cunningham's broader push to bring agentic AI into applied social-science research.
