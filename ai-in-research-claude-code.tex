\begin{abstract}
\noindent This document summarizes the main concepts surrounding the use of
artificial intelligence in research, with a particular focus on
\emph{Claude Code}---an agentic command-line assistant---as a tool for
empirical and quantitative work. Drawing on the extended body of essays
published by economist Scott Cunningham (\href{https://causalinf.substack.com}{Scott's Mixtape Substack}),
it distills a working philosophy in which the researcher--AI \emph{dialogue}
is itself the intellectual work, rather than a mere delivery mechanism. We
cover core capabilities, representative research workflows, best practices,
and the open challenges of reproducibility, verification, and scholarly
credit.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction: AI as a Research Instrument}
Large language models (LLMs) have moved from novelty to infrastructure in the
research process. Rather than serving only as chat interfaces, modern
\emph{agentic} systems can read and write files, run code, execute analyses,
and iterate on results within a project. This shifts the role of AI from a
question-answering oracle toward a \emph{collaborator} embedded in the
researcher's actual environment. The central question is no longer
\emph{whether} AI can assist research, but \emph{how} to integrate it so that
it improves---rather than dilutes---the quality of reasoning.

\section{What Is Claude Code?}
\textbf{Claude Code} is an agentic coding assistant that operates in the
terminal (and via IDE and web integrations). Unlike a passive autocomplete,
it can:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item Explore a codebase or dataset, reading files and searching directories.
  \item Write, edit, and execute scripts, then inspect the output.
  \item Chain multi-step tasks (clean data $\rightarrow$ estimate $\rightarrow$
        visualize $\rightarrow$ write up) with the researcher steering.
  \item Surface ambiguity and ask for direction when the next move is unclear.
\end{itemize}
Its defining feature for research is \emph{iterative, tool-using dialogue}:
the assistant acts within the same environment the researcher inhabits, so its
suggestions are grounded in the actual state of the project.

\section{The Core Philosophy: ``The Dialogue Is the Work''}
A recurring theme in Cunningham's essays is that thinking \emph{through} the
AI---rather than \emph{at} it---is where the value lies:
\begin{quote}
\itshape ``I think more clearly when I am thinking through Claude rather than
at Claude. The dialogue is the work, not just the delivery mechanism for the
work.''
\end{quote}
This implies a deliberately \textbf{lower-automation} stance than many users
adopt. The researcher steers iteratively; the assistant executes when the next
step is clear and pauses to surface ambiguity when it is not. The friction of
articulating a problem clearly enough for the model is not overhead---it is the
reasoning made explicit.

\section{Representative Research Workflows}
\subsection{Empirical / Causal Inference}
Claude Code is well suited to the applied econometrics pipeline: constructing
panels, implementing estimators (e.g.\ difference-in-differences, event
studies, synthetic control), producing diagnostic plots, and drafting the
accompanying prose. Notably, in Cunningham's own audience,
\emph{difference-in-differences} explainers rank among the most valued
content---evidence that AI-assisted methodological writing meets real demand.

\subsection{Reproducible Analysis}
Because the assistant works inside the project directory, it naturally
encourages script-based, re-runnable analysis over ad-hoc manual steps,
supporting reproducibility and version control.

\subsection{Writing and Dissemination}
Beyond code, the same agentic loop extends to authoring: summarizing results,
drafting essays, and---as in the newsletter's own example---integrating with
publishing platforms (e.g.\ connecting Claude Code directly to Substack).

\subsection{Meta-Analysis of One's Own Corpus}
A striking illustration: Cunningham used an AI model to \emph{value his own
essay series}, classifying $\sim$55 posts ($\sim$152{,}000 words) by genre and
estimating their relative social value---a conversational, AI-driven analysis
of the researcher's own output.

\section{Best Practices}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Steer, don't abdicate.} Keep a human in the loop on every
        substantive decision; treat the model's output as a draft to interrogate.
  \item \textbf{Make the dialogue explicit.} Articulating the problem precisely
        is itself analytical work; do not shortcut it.
  \item \textbf{Prefer scripts to manual steps} so analyses remain reproducible
        and auditable.
  \item \textbf{Verify empirically.} Re-run, cross-check estimates, and confirm
        that results reproduce before trusting them.
  \item \textbf{Document the process}, including the prompts and decisions, so
        the provenance of results is clear.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Challenges and Open Questions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Verification.} AI output can be confidently wrong; empirical
        checking is non-negotiable.
  \item \textbf{Reproducibility.} Non-deterministic models complicate exact
        replication; pinning workflows and seeds matters.
  \item \textbf{Attribution and credit.} Norms for acknowledging AI
        contribution in scholarship are still forming.
  \item \textbf{Skill atrophy vs.\ augmentation.} The lower-automation,
        dialogue-first stance is partly a guard against outsourcing the thinking.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}
The most productive framing of AI in research is neither ``autopilot'' nor
``fancy autocomplete,'' but \emph{collaborative dialogue}. Tools like Claude
Code let researchers keep their hands on the analysis while offloading
mechanical friction---accelerating empirical work, reproducible pipelines, and
writing alike. The enduring lesson from practitioners such as Scott Cunningham
is that the value comes from thinking \emph{with} the model, deliberately and
iteratively, so that the conversation itself becomes a form of research.
