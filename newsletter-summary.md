## 🔑 Key Points & Insights

- Connectors are the mechanism Scott hopes will let him use Claude Code from his phone and extend it into cloud services beyond his local machine.
- Connectors live under **Settings → Customize → Connectors** in the Claude desktop app, split into an "installed" view and a "browse/explore" directory.
- The directory holds roughly **4,000 connectors and growing**, with about **451 under the "Code" category** alone.
- Connectors are **not Claude Code–only** — many work from the phone-based Claude app, opening them to "normal" (non–Claude Code) users.
- **MCP (Model Context Protocol)** is an open standard that securely connects AI applications to external data sources and tools for real-time access and actions.
- The directory contains both **Anthropic-originated** connectors and **"Community"** ones that are user-created and only lightly vetted — hence "buyer beware."
- **Financial-services connectors** (Stripe, PayPal, QuickBooks, Gusto payroll, Dropbox) could let an entrepreneur run a small business end-to-end from the chatbot.
- Direct **banking connectors** (e.g., Bank of America) are largely absent, which Scott finds reassuring given data-breach risk; Plaid appears only inconsistently.
- Scott personally uses connectors mainly to **search Dropbox for files from the Claude app** when away from his computer.
- He links connectors to the rise of **"solopreneurs"** — tiny firms (even one person) that Stripe reports are growing fast, some earning **$1M–$10M/year** in revenue.
- He frames this economically: labor-productivity gains feed **per-capita income** — his "wealth of nations" (growth) and "wages of nations" (distribution).
- For **research** specifically, Scott is still unsure connectors are the key tool, but suspects some cloud resources will eventually be reachable only (or most efficiently) via connectors.
- His planned **"harness"** is less a tool than a *staging of his own cognition* — a visual dashboard built around causal-inference design principles.
- The harness is rooted in a **checklist philosophy** that runs through his new book, *Causal Inference: The Remix* (every chapter ends in a checklist, "Practical Tips," or "Practical advice").
- He wants the harness to map into his **Apple Vision Pro**, partly to recover the sense of "seeing" his code that he lost when moving from a text editor to AI agents.
- His current prototype embeds work in **checklist-linked stages** with figures rendered as scrollable, "flippable cards" to help him remember what has been done.
- The whole effort collapses into three goals he repeats: **production, verification, and understanding.**
