## Main topics / themes

- **Connectors in Claude Code** — what they are, where to find them, and how they extend Claude beyond the local machine.
- **The Model Context Protocol (MCP)** — the "piping" that makes connectors work.
- **The "solopreneur" economy** — how connectors could let one person run a real business.
- **Cunningham's "harness" idea** — a visual, checklist-based staging system for causal-inference work.

## Key points / insights (one sentence each)

- Connectors let Cunningham use Claude Code from the Claude phone app and extend it into cloud services rather than just his local computer.
- The connector directory is effectively an "App Store" with ~4,000 connectors (≈451 tagged "Code"), split into Anthropic-made and user-created **Community** ("buyer beware") connectors.
- **MCP (Model Context Protocol)** is an open standard that lets AI apps securely connect to external data and tools and take actions — the key plumbing behind connectors.
- Productivity connectors (e.g., an "Inkbox"-style tool giving an assistant its own email/SMS/iMessage identity) could support a full personal-organization system.
- Financial-services connectors (Stripe, PayPal, QuickBooks, Gusto, Dropbox) could let a solo founder run a small business — though direct **banking** access is notably absent, likely for security.
- He links connectors to the rise of **solo-entrepreneurs** (some reportedly at $1M–$10M revenue), tying labor productivity to per-capita income growth ("the wealth of nations" and "the wages of nations").
- For **research** specifically he's still unsure of the payoff, but expects his eventual causal-inference **harness** to lean on connectors.
- His **harness** is idiosyncratic and checklist-driven (echoing his new book *Causal Inference: The Remix*), aimed at disciplining his cognition around **production, verification, and understanding**.
- He wants the harness to be deeply **visual** — including an Apple Vision Pro interface with "flippable cards" — partly to recover the "seeing" he lost by delegating coding to agents.
