## Main themes

- **Book launch:** *Causal Inference: The Remix* (Yale University Press,
  Aug 25, 2026) — a substantial rewrite of the 2021 *Mixtape*.
- **Open access:** Yale agreed to a free online companion at
  `mixtape.scunning.com`, in the same spirit as the first edition.
- **AI‑native pedagogy:** the online edition ships with materials aimed
  at using an LLM alongside the text, including an essay on how to
  read and study with an LLM, plus links to "skills" and Mixtape
  Sessions workshop material.
- **What's new in the book:** two chapters on modern
  difference‑in‑differences (basics + advanced staggered/complex
  designs), an expanded synthetic control chapter, a reorganised
  unconfoundedness section, more empirical examples, and general
  touch‑ups.

## Key insights (one sentence each)

- The *Remix* is a ~750–784‑page rewrite, not a light second edition.
- Diff‑in‑diff coverage is split across two chapters to separate the
  canonical 2×2 material from staggered adoption, covariate
  adjustment, and modern estimators.
- Synthetic control receives a much longer, unified treatment.
- The unconfoundedness section has been reorganised end‑to‑end.
- Yale agreed to a free online HTML version, keeping the "Mixtape"
  open‑access ethos alive.
- The online edition treats LLM use as a first‑class study aid rather
  than an afterthought.
- Extra "skills" and code snippets are being staged for readers who
  want to work through the book with Claude Code or similar agents.
- The site is a work in progress; more material will land in the
  weeks after the print release.
